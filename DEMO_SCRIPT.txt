--- THE STORY (everyone memorizes this) ---

When someone gets scammed on UPI, the stolen money jumps
through many bank accounts and comes out as CASH from ATMs
within hours. Police usually find out AFTER it is gone.
NIRAKSHAN predicts WHICH ATM area and WHEN - so police can
be there first. It also checks QR codes and scam SMS at the
entry point, and lets any citizen report a scam through a
bot. Every alert is fast, loud, and stays inside what the
court allows.

--- WORDS -> SIMPLE MEANINGS (learn YOUR tab's words) ---

Hawkes process .... "earthquake math": one fraud makes the
                    next fraud nearby MORE likely, like
                    aftershocks. Used to predict the next
                    cash-out area.
Risk score ........ 0-100 number. Higher = cash-out more
                    likely here in this time window.
Hop / layer ....... one bank account the stolen money jumps
                    through. Each jump leaves less evidence.
Spike detector .... if a area gets way more complaints than
                    its own normal level, that's a burst -
                    probably a coordinated attack.
Rule engine ....... a checklist of known scam signs. Each
                    sign adds points. 45+ points = alarm.
Dossier ........... the official PDF report for police,
                    with the court's money limits built in.
Live feed ......... the dashboard updates by itself, like
                    WhatsApp - no refresh needed.
Simulated ......... fake but realistic test data (we say
                    this openly - it builds trust).

Q: Where does your data come from? [M1 or M3]
A: "Three places. The complaint stream is simulated for
   now, because the government NCRP system has no public
   API - our connector plugs into it the day we get access.
   The SMS scam reports are genuinely live - you just saw
   one arrive through the bot. And ATM locations are real,
   from OpenStreetMap. Everything is labeled on screen -
   we never mix the two silently."

Q: Explain that earthquake math. [M2]
A: "It is the Hawkes process. Simple version: when a fraud
   happens, more frauds become likely nearby, soon after -
   like aftershocks. The system keeps a running danger
   score for every area from all past events. The same
   math is used by police departments abroad to predict
   crime hotspots."

Q: Why the numbers 0.42, 1.38, 350 km? [M2]
A: "They match how these gangs actually work: money passes
   2-3 accounts - that is the 42% evidence loss per hop.
   The urgency fades over hours - that is 1.38. And crews
   hit places within a few hundred km - that is the 350 km
   spread. All three are sliders in the System tab - when
   real data flows in, the model recalibrates itself."

Q: Is the spike detection really AI? [M3]
A: "It is statistical AI - a spike detector. Every area
   has a rolling normal level. If complaints suddenly go
   way above it, that is probably a coordinated attack, and
   we flag it. It needs no training data - so it works on
   day one in a brand-new city."

Q: Scammers write 'K Y C' with spaces - then what? [M5 or M1]
A: "Three layers. The rule list catches the simple tricks
   today. Next, the AI text layer we already use for briefs
   handles messy wording. And every genuine bank SMS has a
   verified sender header - checking that kills most fakes
   instantly. All three fit our connector design."

Q: How is this different from what cyber police do now?
   [M1]
A: "Today: complaint comes in, someone freezes accounts,
   hours pass, cash is gone. We flip the order: the system
   predicts where cash will surface in the next 2 to 24
   hours, officers pre-position there, and new scam reports
   alarm in seconds. We turn waiting time into action
   time."

Q: Can you freeze anyone's account? [M1]
A: "No - by design. The system only suggests holding the
   DISPUTED amount, never the whole account. That is the
   Rajasthan High Court rule, and it is written into the
   report generator. There is even a blanket-freeze switch
   in our System tab that shows a big red ILLEGAL warning -
   we built the law into the product."

Q: Will it scale to all of India? [M6 or M1]
A: "The backend is one Python file with zero special
   dependencies - it runs on any government server as-is.
   For national scale, we swap the small database file for
   a big one and run more copies behind a load balancer.
   Standard practice - designed for it from day one."

Q: What did the deployment look like? [M6]
A: "The site is hosted free on Render, connected to our
   GitHub - every push to the main branch redeploys the
   live site automatically in about two minutes. A free
   monitor pings the health page every five minutes so it
   never sleeps. Total running cost: essentially zero."

Q: How much does it cost to run? [M6]
A: "Near zero. No paid tools, no special servers - plain
   Python on any machine. For a police department that
   means it can run inside their own building, on their
   own machines, with the data never leaving their
   network."
