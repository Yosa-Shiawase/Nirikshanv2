--- PART 1: WHAT THIS SYSTEM IS ---

NIRAKSHAN is a website + a brain that helps police catch
digital-fraud money BEFORE it disappears. Four jobs:
  1. LISTEN  - collect fraud reports (live feed + citizens)
  2. PREDICT - guess which ATM areas get hit next
  3. WATCH   - scan QR codes and SMS for scam patterns
  4. REPORT  - make the legal paperwork automatically

It runs from ONE Python file (app.py) on a free cloud
server. The website is plain files - no heavy frameworks -
so it can run on ANY police computer with nothing
installed.

--- PART 2: THE WEBSITE - TAB BY TAB ---

Always visible top-right: INGEST counter (live complaints
so far), DATA: LIVE dot, RUN LIVE SIMULATION (5-step
auto-tour - emergency demo only).

1) RISK MAP - the main screen
   - Map of India. Each glowing dot = an ATM cluster area
     we track (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru...).
   - Dot color = danger: green -> yellow -> orange -> red.
   - Bottom slider = time window (+2h/+6h/+24h). Drag it:
     scores change. That IS the prediction.
   - Tap a dot: right panel shows that area's full report
     card (score, badge, reasons, decision trail).
   - Top-left layers icon: dark ops / satellite / streets.
   - ATM LAYER (LIVE OSM) button: drops REAL ATM pins
     around the most dangerous area. First click slow
     (real internet query), then cached 6 hours.
   - Blue pulse = a complaint just arrived there.
     Red pulse + banner = sudden spike (burst).

2) QR ANALYSIS
   - Camera reads any UPI QR. The checklist asks: scammy
     name ("refund", "cashback")? real bank handle? amount
     pre-filled to rush you? on our watchlist?
   - Verdict: LIKELY LEGIT / SAFE WITH CAUTION /
     FLAGGED_FRAUD_RISK + exact reasons.
   - FRAUD/LEGIT DEMO buttons show a scannable QR on
     screen (for another phone to scan).

3) ALERTS
   - Top card: LIVE SMS THREAT SENSOR. Paste any SMS ->
     ANALYZE -> instant scored verdict. Sample buttons:
     FRAUD / LOTTERY / BENIGN.
   - Below: the alert feed. Live bot reports land here.
   - Score 45+: red banner drops + siren + phone buzzes.

4) ENTITIES
   - One suspect's network: his phones, devices, bank
     accounts as connected circles. Demo data (labeled) -
     shows how a suspect would be tracked.

5) TRANSACTIONS
   - Two things on this tab:
     TOP: ACTIVE CASE TRACE card - one stolen amount
     (Rs 3,80,000) split across laundering layers:
       H0 victim -> H1 splitters -> H2 aggregators ->
       H3 cash-out ATMs (DL-01 / MUM-01 / BLR-01).
     The ATM legs are REAL terminal IDs - when live
     complaints hit them, the leg turns red:
     "LIVE ... INTERCEPT NOW". Upper layers (bank
     accounts) show "awaiting live activity" - they fill
     when account-level data flows in (the bank-API stage
     of the project; the adapter is ready).
     BELOW: the money-trail graph (victim -> mules ->
     ATM). Each arrow shows the amount and the evidence
     left: 42%, then 18%, then 7.4% (0.42 per hop).
     Click a circle: details + the legal hold limit.
   - This tab answers: "how does the stolen money move,
     and where do we catch it?"

6) DASHBOARD
   - The live table - every complaint appears here the
     second it arrives (no refresh). Plus 4 KPI cards and
     regional risk bars. Best tab to PROVE it is alive.

7) REPORTS
   - Report archive + preview card + the PDF generator
     (also reachable via EXPORT REPORT bottom-right).

8) SYSTEM
   - 5 color themes. The three math sliders (do not touch
     on stage). The legal toggle: strict mode (correct) vs
     blanket-freeze (shows a red ILLEGAL warning - built-in
     law lesson). SSO session card (demo identity).

RIGHT PANEL (Zone 3, always visible):
   The selected area's case card: risk score, badge, time
   window, risk factor pills, the 5-step decision trail
   (WHY we think this), the legal freeze limit. Bottom
   buttons: EXPORT REPORT makes the PDF.

--- PART 3: THE THREE ENGINES (simple) ---

ENGINE 1 - PREDICTOR (the "earthquake math")
  Every past complaint pushes UP the danger of nearby
  areas for a while, then it fades. Three knobs:
    time fade: 1.38 per hour (how fast excitement dies)
    distance:  350 km (how far it spreads)
    hop decay: 0.42 (each account hop leaves 42% of the
               evidence - money gets "diluted")
  Output: a 0-100 score per area per time window. Top
  scores = police should pre-position there.

ENGINE 2 - SPIKE DETECTOR
  Each area keeps a rolling "normal level". If complaints
  jump past 5 in 30 seconds AND 2.2x its own normal ->
  coordinated burst -> red alert banner. No training data
  needed - works day one in any new city.

ENGINE 3 - CHECKLISTS (QR + SMS)
  Known scam signs, each adds points:
    SMS: lottery +30, KYC scare +25, OTP asking +25,
         remote-app +25, refund trick +20, short link +20,
         fake authority +15, urgency +10,
         foreign +92 sender +25, number-only sender +10.
    QR:  watchlist +60, unknown bank handle +15,
         scam words +45, pre-filled amount +15 (+10 big).
  Totals: 45+ = ALARM, 20-44 = watch quietly, below 20 =
  just log. Every point has a printed reason - the system
  can always explain WHY it alarmed.

--- PART 4: WHERE DATA COMES FROM (be exact) ---

LIVE (genuinely real):
  - Citizen scam reports via Telegram bot -> scored ->
    alarms -> verdict reply to the reporter.
  - ATM/bank locations: OpenStreetMap around the danger
    area.
  - QR verdicts: rule engine on whatever the camera reads.

SIMULATED (openly labeled):
  - The complaint feed (NCRP has no public API yet; our
    generator copies real patterns: metros heavier,
    occasional coordinated bursts).
  - Suspect cards and the demo money-trail numbers.

HONEST RULE: never mix the two silently. The label IS the
credibility.

--- PART 5: THE ALERT CHAIN (step by step) ---

A citizen texts the bot a scam SMS:
  1. Telegram delivers it to our server (listening 24/7)
  2. Checklist engine scores it (e.g. 55/100 = ALERT)
  3. Saved to the database (evidence)
  4. Dashboard: red banner + siren + new row
  5. Operator's phone: Telegram push, buzzes till touched
  6. Reporter gets a reply: "Received. Verdict: FRAUD
     ALERT, risk 55. Rules: kyc, bit.ly. Forwarded."
  Time: a few seconds. No human touched anything.
  (The ALERTS-tab paste box does the same for SMS that
  arrive by other means - operator intake.)

--- PART 6: THE LEGAL BACKBONE ---

Rajasthan High Court rule (Aug 2026): hold ONLY the
disputed amount - never freeze whole accounts.
We enforce it in software:
  - every report caps the hold at the disputed value
  - the illegal blanket-freeze mode shows a red ILLEGAL
    warning in System tab
  - dossiers cite the mandate automatically
This is our differentiator: proactive BUT constitutional.

--- PART 8: HOSTING STUFF ---

- Free host: Render.com, connected to GitHub. Every push to
  main = live site updates itself in ~2 min (say "CI/CD").
- UptimeRobot pings the /health page every 5 min -> server
  never dozes (a sleeping server would waste 50 seconds).
- Database: one SQLite file. Prototype-fine; national scale
  swaps to PostgreSQL - one connection change.
- Secret keys live ONLY in Render settings (never in code):
  Telegram bot token + chat id, ntfy topic (bonus channel),
  optional Gemini AI key.

--- PART 9: NUMBERS CHEAT SHEET (memorize 6) ---

  0.42     evidence left per money hop
  1.38/hr  fraud excitement fade speed
  350 km   contagion spread distance
  45/100   alarm threshold (SMS and QR)
  2.2x     spike vs normal = coordinated burst
  2-24h    the prediction windows

--- PART 10: THINGS THAT CAN GO WRONG ---

Site slow on arrival  -> cold start; monitor prevents it;
                         we also open it 10 min early.
ATM pins slow          -> first query warms a 6h cache.
Telegram push late     -> console siren is the show; push
                         is the backup channel.
Case trace upper layers "awaiting" -> account legs fill
                         when bank-account data flows (next
                         integration stage); ATM legs are
                         the live-matched ones today.
Judge asks something odd -> "here is how we would approach
                         it..." + one sentence. Never invent
                         numbers.
